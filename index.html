Advice on disability equality law for organisations that want to get it right

EquaLex is a consultancy based in Zurich. We help businesses, public bodies and non-governmental organisations understand their obligations under disability equality law and turn them into practical, meaningful compliance.

Services

  • Legal and compliance advice

    Reviewing policies, products and services against disability equality law, and advising on the steps needed for compliance that works for disabled people in practice.

  • Policy and organisational consulting

    Advising public bodies and NGOs on the design and application of disability law and policy, including project management for implementation.

  • Training

    Training for judges, prosecutors, legal professionals and staff on disability law, with particular attention to the requirements of international law.

  • The disability market

    Helping businesses understand disabled people as customers and employees, and build accessibility into how they operate.

  • Corporate services

    In addition to consultancy, EquaLex accepts fiduciary mandates for companies. Please contact us to discuss your requirements.

About

Richard Whittle founded EquaLex after a long career as a legal academic in the UK, specialising in EU equality law, comparative disability law and international human rights law and policy. He has lectured in law since 1995, most recently as Senior Lecturer at Sheffield Hallam University.

Throughout that time he has advised public bodies and NGOs at national and international level on the design and application of disability law, and advised businesses on meaningful compliance. His roles have included:

  • Expert adviser to the British Disability Rights Commission (now the Equality and Human Rights Commission)
  • Expert adviser to the European Disability Forum
  • Member of the European Commission’s group of experts on combating discrimination on grounds of disability
  • Assisting in the drafting of the first European Union law prohibiting disability discrimination

Richard regularly trains members of the judiciary and senior public prosecutors on disability law, and lectures on the disability programmes of the Academy of European Law (ERA).
